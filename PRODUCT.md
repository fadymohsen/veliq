# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Business owners and decision-makers in Egypt, Saudi Arabia, UAE, and the US evaluating a web development / SEO agency for a new site build or ongoing support. They're comparing agencies, often mid-research (reading blog content, service pages, case studies) before booking a discovery call. Secondary audience: existing clients checking in on services/pricing.

## Product Purpose

VELIQ is a web development and SEO agency marketing site. It exists to establish credibility, explain services (website development, website support, SEO), showcase project work, and convert visitors into discovery-call bookings or contact form submissions. The blog is a content-marketing surface: it should demonstrate technical/SEO expertise and pull qualified traffic from organic search, not just exist as a checkbox feature.

## Brand Personality

Confident, technical, premium. Precise and credible rather than playful. Think dark, minimal, high-craft software-company marketing (Linear/Vercel-style) rather than loud startup energy or generic agency friendliness. The site should read as "we build fast, technically excellent websites" through its own execution, not just its copy.

## Anti-references

Avoid the generic SaaS-template / WordPress-agency-blog look: stock gradient thumbnail cards, identical icon+heading+text grids, cookie-cutter "10 tips" listicle styling, decorative gradient text, side-stripe accent borders. Nothing that could be mistaken for an unmodified theme.

## Design Principles

- Practice what you preach: as a web dev/SEO agency, the site's own performance, structure, and polish are part of the pitch — reading experience and technical execution matter as much as visual style.
- Confidence through restraint: precision and typographic hierarchy carry the design, not decoration or heavy color.
- Content is the product on the blog: optimize for actual reading (line length, rhythm, scannability) over decorative card grids.
- Consistent system: components (cards, pills, CTAs) should feel like one deliberate system across blog, services, and projects, not one-off page styling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No stated WCAG target; default to solid AA contrast on the dark theme (already using near-white text on near-black, indigo accent rgb(99,102,241) which passes AA on black). No specific reduced-motion or color-blindness requirements raised; keep existing Framer Motion animations subtle and respect prefers-reduced-motion where added.
